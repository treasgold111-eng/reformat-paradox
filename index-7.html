Scan text from images

Get the exact text from photos, screenshots and scanned pages. Choose several images at once. They’re read on your device and never uploaded.

Line breaks

Needs internet the first time, to download the reader and the language. After that your browser may keep them for offline use. Tap an image to zoom in and check the text against it. Pictures are tidied up automatically in the background to make the text easier to read.

Turn text into an image

Type or paste text and save it as a picture. Use a colour or your own picture behind it.

The text is taller than the picture. Make it smaller, or choose Fit to text.

Drag the text to move it. Pinch to resize it. On a computer, hold Ctrl or Shift and scroll over the picture.

Make a PDF

Turn text or a stack of images into one PDF. It is built on your device and works offline. Every page is shown separately so you can tap it and zoom in.

This text has characters the standard PDF fonts can’t hold (for example Arabic, Chinese or emoji). Each page will be saved as an image, so the text won’t be selectable.
