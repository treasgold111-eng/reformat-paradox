Privacy Policy

Reformat Paradox · Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short: Reformat Paradox works on your own device. The images, text and PDFs you use are not uploaded to us, and we do not run any servers that receive them. We do not ask you to create an account. The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data described below.

1. Your files stay on your device

When you convert images, scan text from pictures, make text cards, build PDFs or add captions, all of the work happens on your phone or computer. Nothing you choose is sent to the developer. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information ourselves.

Files you save are stored on your device, in the Documents folder for the Android app or your browser's download location for the website. You can delete them at any time.

2. Content downloaded from other servers

To work, the app loads some public resources from other companies' servers, such as fonts from Google Fonts and, the first time you use text scanning, the open-source text-recognition engine and its language data from public content delivery servers (for example cdnjs.cloudflare.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net and tessdata.projectnaptha.com). Like any website, these servers can see technical details of the request, such as your IP address and browser or device type. They never receive your images or text.

3. Ads in the Android app

The Android app shows banner ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, including your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this personal data and do not have access to it.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. In regions where the law requires it, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, which can vary by device).

The website version of Reformat Paradox does not show ads.

4. The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as visitor IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not use analytics or tracking tools ourselves. The website saves a copy of its own files in your browser so that it can work offline.

5. Children

Reformat Paradox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete or export. Uninstalling the app or clearing the website data in your browser removes everything the app keeps on your device. For ad data held by Google, use the controls described in section 3.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/treasgold111-eng/reformat-paradox/issues.
